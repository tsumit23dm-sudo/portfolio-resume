## 🎯 HOW TO CHOOSE?

### **Choose LIGHT Theme if:**
- ✅ You're applying to traditional marketing roles
- ✅ Your target company is corporate/established
- ✅ You prefer a classic, professional look
- ✅ You'll print your resume on paper
- ✅ You want maximum compatibility

### **Choose DARK Theme if:**
- ✅ You're applying to tech/startup roles
- ✅ Your target company is modern/innovative
- ✅ You want to stand out visually
- ✅ You'll submit digitally (PDF/Email)
- ✅ You want a trendy appearance
