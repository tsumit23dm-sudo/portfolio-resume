# 📋 Section Customization Guide

> **Rearrange resume sections to match your strengths and the job description**

---

## 🎯 Why Section Order Matters?

**Recruiters spend only 6-7 seconds scanning a resume!** Put your strongest sections first to grab their attention immediately.

---

## 🔀 AVAILABLE SECTIONS (Drag & Drop Order)

Here are all the sections included in your resume templates. You can **reorder them** based on your situation:

1. **📌 Professional Summary**
2. **💼 Professional Experience**
3. **🎯 Key Skills**
4. **🎓 Education**
5. **🏆 Notable Achievements & Projects**
6. **📚 Additional Information**
7. **🔗 Portfolio/Links Section** *(Optional)*

## 🎯 HOW TO CHOOSE YOUR SECTION ORDER?

### **Ask Yourself These Questions:**

1. **What is my strongest asset?**
   - Skills? → Put Skills section early
   - Experience? → Keep Experience section early
   - Achievements/Portfolio? → Move Achievements to top

2. **What does the job description emphasize?**
   - Required skills listed first? → List Skills early
   - Years of experience needed? → Keep Experience prominent
   - Portfolio/samples wanted? → Add Achievements section early

3. **What's my career stage?**
   - Entry-level? → Skills & Achievements first
   - Mid-career? → Traditional order (Experience first)
   - Senior/Leadership? → Experience & Achievements first
   - Career changer? → Skills & Achievements first

4. **What industry am I applying to?**
   - Traditional (Finance, Law, Corporate) → Traditional order
   - Tech/Startup → Skills & Achievements first
   - Creative (Design, Marketing, Social Media) → Portfolio/Achievements first

## 🔧 HOW TO CUSTOMIZE YOUR TEMPLATE

### **Step 1: Download Your Template**
Choose Light or Dark theme and download the markdown file.

### **Step 2: Identify Your Strongest Section**
- What makes you stand out most?
- What's most relevant to the job?

### **Step 3: Reorder the Sections**
Simply cut and paste sections in your markdown editor to rearrange them.

**Example:**
```markdown
# [YOUR NAME]

## 📌 PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY
[Your summary text]

## 🏆 NOTABLE ACHIEVEMENTS & PROJECTS
[Your achievements - moved to position 2!]

## 🎯 KEY SKILLS
[Your skills]

## 💼 PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE
[Your experience]

## 🎓 EDUCATION
[Your education]

## 📚 ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
[Additional info]
```

### **Step 4: Keep the Emojis & Headers**
- Keep emoji icons for visual appeal ✨
- Keep header formatting (##, ###) intact
- Maintain consistent styling

---

## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR SECTION ORDERING

✅ **DO:**
- Match section order to job description
- Put 2-3 strongest sections first
- Keep summary always first (it's an overview)
- Test different orders for different companies

❌ **DON'T:**
- Bury your best achievements
- Randomly shuffle sections
- Make education first (unless applying to academic roles)
- Over-complicate the order

## 📝 BEFORE & AFTER EXAMPLES

### **Example 1: Junior Social Media Manager**

**BEFORE (Traditional Order):**
```
1. Summary
2. Experience (2 years)
3. Skills
4. Education
5. Achievements
```

**AFTER (Better for Junior):**
```
1. Summary

3. Achievements (show campaigns & results)
4. Experience (2 years but with metrics)
```
